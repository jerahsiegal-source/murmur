# Intent: Yappr product video

## Outcome
One finished 30–60 second widescreen (16:9) product video for Yappr exists as a file ready to post on a website. It shows the real app doing what it does, and looks and sounds like a professional product video.

## Why
A website visitor should understand within a minute what Yappr is and think it's cool: an open source Wispr Flow and Speechify merged into one app, running on your own API key. The video is there to show off the product and how it works. When a tradeoff comes up, favour "a stranger gets it and is impressed" over completeness; it is a showcase, not a tutorial.

## Done when (all must pass)
- [ ] One video file, 16:9, between 30 and 60 seconds, in a format a website can play — evidence: file path plus its reported duration, resolution and format.
- [ ] Shows dictation: speaking and the words appearing where the cursor is, in a real app, the way Wispr Flow works — evidence: watch the video; timestamp of the scene.
- [ ] Shows read aloud: highlighting text and Yappr reading it back, audibly — evidence: watch with sound on; timestamp.
- [ ] Shows pasting an API key into Yappr to set it up — evidence: watch; timestamp; the key on screen is a dummy.
- [ ] States the pitch outright, naming both: an open source Wispr Flow + Speechify in one — evidence: appears on screen and in the voiceover; timestamp.
- [ ] Footage is the real Yappr app, recorded working, polished with branded titles, zooms and transitions — evidence: every app scene matches how the current app actually looks and behaves.
- [ ] Sound: background music plus an AI voiceover, with captions so it still makes sense muted — evidence: watch once with sound, once muted.
- [ ] Branding: the app's own colour scheme, the Yappr logo and Skip the dog — evidence: stills from the video side by side with the app.
- [ ] Looks professional: no jitter, typos, stray windows, notifications, personal data or dead air — evidence: a still from every scene checked, then owner review of the full video.
- [ ] End to end: someone who has never seen Yappr can say after one viewing what it does and that it is open source — evidence: owner watches it start to finish and agrees.
- [ ] Nothing in the app or repo is broken or changed in behaviour by making the video — evidence: existing tests still pass; working tree shows only video assets added.
Partial completion is not done. If any item cannot be met, stop and report which and why.

## Never
- Show a real API key, or the owner's real email, calendar, notes or other personal data.
- Show Yappr doing something it does not actually do, or fake a feature.
- Use music, voices, fonts or footage that are not licensed for public website use.
- Quit, disable or reconfigure Wispr Flow on this machine without asking (it shares Yappr's hotkeys and will react during recording).
- Post or publish the video anywhere; the owner posts it.

## Stop and ask before
- Spending any money (music, voice, stock assets, tools, paid services).
- Anything that needs the owner at the keyboard or microphone, or needs Wispr Flow turned off.
- Calling it final: the owner reviews the full video first.

## Yours to decide
Everything not listed above: script and wording, scene order, pacing, which apps the dictation is shown in, whether to include extras such as meeting notes, the music and voice chosen, tools, file names. Prefer what best serves the Why.
